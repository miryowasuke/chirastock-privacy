# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** January 4, 2026

Chirastock (“the App”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

## 1. Information we collect

### 1.1 Account information

- Email address
- Password (stored encrypted)
- Username

### 1.2 Inventory information

- Item names
- Item photos (optional)
- Stock status
- Store URLs (optional)
- Notes (optional)

### 1.3 Group information

- Group names
- Group member information

### 1.4 Technical information

- Device information
- App usage (crash reports and performance data)

## 2. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

- Create and authenticate accounts
- Manage and share inventory
- Provide group features
- Improve the App and fix bugs
- Provide user support

## 3. Sharing

We do not share personal information with third parties except:

- When you give explicit consent
- When required by law
- When needed to provide the service (for example Firebase and other providers)

## 4. Storage and security

- Data is stored on Firebase (Google Cloud Platform)
- Credentials are stored encrypted
- Firestore security rules restrict access to authenticated users
- Access is also controlled per group

## 5. Your rights

You may:

- Access your personal information
- Correct your personal information
- Delete your personal information
- Delete your account

To exercise these rights, delete your account in Settings or contact us.

## 6. Photo library access

The App may request access to your photo library so you can choose item photos. We use this permission only after you grant it.

## 7. Retention

We keep data until you delete your account. After deletion, data is fully removed within 30 days.

## 8. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy. If we make material changes, we will notify you in the App.
